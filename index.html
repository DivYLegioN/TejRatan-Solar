12-Month Simulation (Apr → Mar)

Each row is its own month: generation comes from the GSA profile for that month, the bank balance carries forward from the row above (clamped at ≥ 0, exactly like BankingEngine.process_month), and bills/revenue/expense/profit are computed fresh for that month. Whenever generation + bank can't fully cover this consumer's contracted solar units, the uncovered units are billed separately as a Shortfall Bill at the government tariff — they don't just disappear from the total, so savings in low-generation months come out realistic instead of inflated. The Annual Total row is the real sum of these 12 rows — not one month multiplied by 12.
